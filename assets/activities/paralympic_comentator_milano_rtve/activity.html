Recently, I had the opportunity to participate as a commentator on the national Spanish television (Radiotelevisión Española (RTVE)) for the cross-country skiing and biathlon events at the Milan 2026 Paralympic Games, covering the debut of Higinio Rivero: the first Spaniard to compete in three different sports at the Paralympic Games, the first Spaniard to compete in Paralympic biathlon, and the first Spaniard to compete in seated cross-country skiing.

This experience, which comes from my background as a cross-country skiing and biathlon athlete for many years, has been extremely enriching. It allowed me to learn about and closely understand how the media operates, working alongside the outstanding sports journalist Julia Luna, to whom I will be eternally grateful.

As a specialist in artificial intelligence, and motivated by the short time available to prepare for this task, I decided to support my research and preparation on the current Paralympic landscape in each category using a multi-agent system. This made it easier to gather information and stories about each athlete more quickly, allowing me to obtain easily structured summaries in table format, where each row referred to a different athlete.

This system, based on the CrewAI tool, enabled me to create a complete profile of each athlete by collecting information from both official sources and specific press articles about each of them. Once all this information had been gathered, I stored and structured it into multiple markdown files that I could use as reference material during the live broadcast of the Games directly from my GitHub.

The results served as an initial foundation which—although interpreted with caution due to its room for improvement, since I was only able to dedicate three/four afternoons to developing the system—allowed me to prepare for the Games much more efficiently and access information about each athlete much more quickly.

It has been a wonderful experience in which we were able to enjoy the performances of these athletes, highlighting the tremendous effort made by our representative, Higinio Rivero.
