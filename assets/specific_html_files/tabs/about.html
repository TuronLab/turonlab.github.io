About Me

Hi! I'm Pablo Turón, an AI Engineer passionate about applying technology to solve real-world problems. I studied Telecommunications and Technology Engineering at the University of Zaragoza, where I discovered my passion for turning innovation into practical solutions—even exploring how technology could enhance my own performance as a high-level athlete (see the story here).

My engineering thesis marked the beginning of my AI journey through an internship at Sallén Tech. I developed a computer vision system to recognise banknote patterns that achieved the company's accuracy goals in just one-third of the usual computation time, even on low-resource hardware. This experience sparked my interest in applied AI and led me to pursue an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the Polytechnic University of Madrid.

During my master's, I entered the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP), collaborating with doctors at Hospital 12 de Octubre to develop systems that automatically identified and classified medical entities in clinical records, helping extract valuable insights from complex healthcare data.

From 2020 to June 2026, I was part of the Vicomtech NLP research team, where I worked on applied AI development, bridging the gap between research and real-world solutions. I contributed to both European research projects and industry-driven initiatives, developing technologies in areas such as Information Extraction, Text Classification, Named Entity Recognition (NER), Document Understanding, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). My work covered the full development cycle, from exploring new AI methodologies to designing and deploying reliable systems adapted to client needs, combining traditional Machine Learning techniques with recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs). Along the way, I contributed to scientific publications in peer-reviewed journals and presented research at international conferences (check out my Google Scholar profile for details).

Currently, I am working at Baiara, a new artificial intelligence consultancy focused on building innovative AI technologies and creating solutions that can be applied across different industries. In this new role, I combine my technical background with a stronger leadership focus, helping shape AI strategies, develop new products and services, and drive the adoption of AI in real-world environments.
